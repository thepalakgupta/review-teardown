Source: 7,467 public Google Play Store reviews of the STAGE app (2 Jan 2020 – 10 Oct 2026). Focus period: the last 12 months, 11 Oct 2025 – 10 Oct 2026. Written October 2026.

The rating fell mostly because short 5★ reviews dried up, but reviews with real content turned more negative too. Most complaints trace back to one journey: a ₹1 trial that becomes a ₹199 autopay that users did not expect, cannot easily stop, and sometimes cannot even watch. Users love the content itself, so clearer billing and reliable access are the fastest route back.

Much of the fall comes from fewer short "good" / "nice" reviews. 5★ reviews dropped from about 34 a day to 3–5 a day, while 1★ reviews stayed at about 3–5 a day until July. But the decline is not only about volume. Among reviews with more than 3 words, the average fell from 3.44 (Oct–Jan) to 2.22 (Feb–Oct).

Question for the team: did the in-app rating prompt change around Jan–Feb 2026? That would explain part of the drop in short 5★ reviews.

The three issues that matter most

1. The ₹1 trial becomes a ₹199 autopay users didn't expect

  • 509 reviews (54%) of substantive complaints are about an unexpected auto-debit or the ₹1 trial.
  • Unexpected auto-debit is the biggest theme at 44%, and it stays near that level all year. It is not tied to any app version.
  • It is long-standing: the same "they said ₹1, then took ₹199" complaint appears in Hindi reviews from Nov 2023.
  • Hinglish writers are over-represented (57% of auto-debit complaints vs 48% of all complaints). 46% of these reviews have no app version, so many were probably written after uninstalling.
"They took my money. They said ₹1 would be cut, but they cut ₹199." (translated from Hinglish)

Users treat the ₹1 as the whole deal. The fix is to make the ₹199 that follows impossible to miss.

2. "I paid and still can't watch"

Share of substantive complaints tagged "paid but can't watch", by month. Oct 2025 (35 complaints) and Oct 2026 (10) are small partial months.

  • 185 reviews (20%) in total. They make up 33% of complaints in May–Jul 2026, against 13% in the rest of the window.
  • One rule comes up again and again: turning off autopay ends the trial the user already paid for. At least 20 reviews say this explicitly, and 28 say the app still showed "not subscribed" after payment.
  • In June, the theme appeared 15.2 times per 100 reviews on 5.x builds vs 5.8 on 4.x builds. New users also install the newest build, so this is a lead to check, not proof.
  • 70% of these reviewers still had the app installed. They are paying users who want to watch.
"Already took trial paid 1rs and when i cancelled the autopay option than i am not able to see any content."

Question for the team: what changed in subscription or entitlement logic between mid-May and mid-June 2026, and was something fixed in late July?

3. Hard to stop autopay, and replies that redirect instead of resolve

  • 119 reviews (13%) say they were charged after cancelling or uninstalling, or couldn't find how to cancel. The share rose from 10.6% (Oct 2025–Apr 2026) to 16.5% (Aug–Oct 2026).
  • 44% of these reviewers no longer have the app. Many seem to believe that uninstalling stops the mandate.
  • Every complaint gets a public reply, typically within half an hour. That is a real strength. But only 22% of replies to these reviews include cancel steps, and only 10% of refund complaints get a reply that commits to a refund outcome.
  • The support route in replies changed three times in a year (phone → in-app chat → email). Users who have uninstalled can't reach in-app chat.
"It does not allow to cancel my subscription… your money will be deducted everytime."

What users love

Content is STAGE's strength. Only 5% of complaints are about the library. Among 769 substantive 4–5★ reviews, the specific praise is about:

  • Regional language and culture pride (97 reviews): Haryanvi, Rajasthani and Bhojpuri stories in "my own language".
  • Stories with emotion or a social message (91 reviews): "heart touching", real-life issues.
  • Named titles (48 reviews). Savari has 16 positive mentions vs 3 negative; Jholachhap has 15 vs 0.
"You've brought Haryanvi culture back to life." (translated from Hindi)
"I installed this app only to view Khejdi. And I love it."

People like what they watch. The work is in how they pay for it.

Methodology & limitations

  • Data. Public Play Store reviews, 2 Jan 2020 – 10 Oct 2026. Data before 23 Oct 2025 comes only from a Hindi-language scrape, so the rating trend starts on 23 Oct 2025. Themes use 11 Oct 2025 – 10 Oct 2026.
  • Base. "Substantive" means more than 3 words. 69% of all reviews are 3 words or fewer; they are counted but not themed. Hindi, Hinglish and Devanagari reviews are included, and quotes are translated where needed.
  • Themes. Tagged with multilingual keyword rules and checked by reading random samples. One review can carry several themes. "Money themes" = unexpected auto-debit, ₹1 trial, paid but can't watch, can't cancel, refund, and paywall & price: 644 of 948 reviews (68%).
  • Trend definition. The "paid but can't watch" figures compare May–Jul 2026 with all other months of the 12-month window.
  • Reviewers are not all users. Very happy and very angry people post more. These figures show what reviewers raise, not how many users are affected. STAGE's billing data can size each issue.
  • Version is not cause. App version tracks calendar time and user type, so version gaps are leads to check. Reply analysis shows what was said publicly, not what was resolved.
